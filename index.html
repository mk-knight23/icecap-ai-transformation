IceCap offers Bridge/FixNFlip® loans, DSCR/Term loans (30-year), and Ground-Up construction loans. We also offer portfolio loans for rental investors and short-term bridge loans. All products are for non-owner-occupied residential investment properties.

Bridge Loans can close in a few days — typically under 2 weeks. Fix & Flip and DSCR Loans generally close within 2 weeks. Ground-Up Construction Loans typically close within 2–3 weeks. Our CTC process can be completed in as little as 24 hours.

No — IceCap Group exclusively finances non-owner occupied investment properties. Our programs are business-purpose only. Tenants may occupy the property, but the borrower cannot use it as their primary residence.

We have a dedicated Broker Program (TPO) for mortgage brokers and referral partners. Most of our loans are originated via broker partnerships. You can work through our BluFin wholesale platform or on a deal-by-deal referral basis. Approvals typically complete within 1 business day.

We finance residential investment properties including single-family homes (1–4 units), condos, townhomes, duplexes, small multifamily, and mixed-use properties. We focus on non-owner occupied residential real estate in all 50+ states where IceCap is licensed.
